# Wholemeal Wheat Flour Pizza Dough

One serving(~200g dough) should be enough to make a pizza.
The dough can be stored in the refrigerator for ~2 days.
For it to last longer, it should be stored in the freezer. ⏲️ Prep time: 70 min 🍽️ Servings: 4 Ingredients 500g flour (Wholemeal Wheat Flour) 280ml lukewarm water 8g dry yeast 3.5 spoon olive oil 1.5 teaspoon salt Directions Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and knead into a dough. Let it rest for ~60 minutes somewhere lukewarm.
