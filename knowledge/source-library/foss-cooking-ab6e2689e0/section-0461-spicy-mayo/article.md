# Spicy Mayo

A simple sauce that is at almost every sushi bar in the United States. ⏲️ Prep time: 3 min Ingredients chili sauce (e.g Sriracha) mayonnaise sesame oil Directions Add 2 parts mayonnaise to 1 part chili sauce in a bowl. Add 1 tbsp of sesame oil for every cup of mayonnaise you use. Mix all of the ingredients together, and taste test it for more chili sauce or mayonnaise. Note This is just a basic guide, adapt this recipe to your liking.
