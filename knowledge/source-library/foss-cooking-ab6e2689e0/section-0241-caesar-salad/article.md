# Caesar Salad

Caesar Salad is an easy and delicious meal for lunch or dinner. Ingredients Romaine Lettuce Grated Parmesan Cheese Croutons Caesar Salad Dressing Directions Prepare a whole head of Romaine Lettuce by chopping off the root and rising thoroughly with water. Dry completely. Once the lettuce is fully dry, put in a mixing bowl with lettuce and the Caesar Salad dressing. Toss to cover each leaf completely. Add croutons. Grate Parmesan over salad. Eat with hands.
