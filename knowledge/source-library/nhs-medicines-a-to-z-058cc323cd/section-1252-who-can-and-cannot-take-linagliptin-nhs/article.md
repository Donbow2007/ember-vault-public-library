# Who can and cannot take linagliptin - NHS

Who can and cannot take linagliptin

Who can take linagliptin Most adults aged 18 years and older can take linagliptin. Who may not be able to take linagliptin This medicine is not used to treat type 1 diabetes (when your body does not produce insulin). Linagliptin is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to linagliptin or any other medicine have ever had problems with your pancreas are pregnant or breastfeeding, or trying to get pregnant
