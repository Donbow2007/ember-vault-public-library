# Who can and cannot take short-acting insulin - NHS

Who can and cannot take short-acting insulin - Brand names: Actrapid, Humulin S Who can take short-acting insulin Most adults and children with diabetes can take short-acting insulin. Who may not be able to take short-acting insulin Short-acting insulin may not be suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you've ever had an allergic reaction to insulin or any other medicine.
