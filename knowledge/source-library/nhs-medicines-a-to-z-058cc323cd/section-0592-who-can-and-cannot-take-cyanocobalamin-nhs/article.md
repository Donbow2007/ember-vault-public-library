# Who can and cannot take cyanocobalamin - NHS

Who can and cannot take cyanocobalamin - Brand name: Orobalin Who can take cyanocobalamin Most adults and children can take cyanocobalamin. Who may not be able to take cyanocobalamin Cyanocobalamin is not suitable for everyone. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor before starting to take it if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to cyanocobalamin or any other medicine have an allergy to cobalt
