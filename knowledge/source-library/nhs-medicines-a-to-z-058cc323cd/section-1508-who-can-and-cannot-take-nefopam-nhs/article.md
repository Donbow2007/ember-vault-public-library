# Who can and cannot take nefopam - NHS

Who can and cannot take nefopam - Brand name: Acupan Who can take nefopam Most adults can take nefopam. Who may not be able to take nefopam Do not take nefopam for pain caused by a heart attack. Do not take nefopam if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to nefopam or any other medicine have epilepsy, or have ever had a seizure or fit Nefopam is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you: have liver or kidney problems have ever had difficulty peeing have an uncommon kind of glaucoma (increased pressure in the eye) called angle closure glaucoma are trying to get pregnant, are already pregnant or breastfeeding
