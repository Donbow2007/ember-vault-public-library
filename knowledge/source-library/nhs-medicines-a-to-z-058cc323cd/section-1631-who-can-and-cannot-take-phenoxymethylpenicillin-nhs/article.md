# Who can and cannot take phenoxymethylpenicillin - NHS

Who can and cannot take phenoxymethylpenicillin

Who can take phenoxymethylpenicillin Most adults and children can take phenoxymethylpenicillin, including during pregnancy and while breastfeeding. Who may not be able to take phenoxymethylpenicillin Phenoxymethylpenicillin is not suitable for some people. To make sure this medicine is safe for you, tell your doctor if you have: ever had an allergic reaction to phenoxymethylpenicillin, or any other antibiotics or medicine kidney problems any allergies asthma
