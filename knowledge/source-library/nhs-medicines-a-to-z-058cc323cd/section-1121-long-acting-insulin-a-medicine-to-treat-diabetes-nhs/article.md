# Long-acting insulin: a medicine to treat diabetes - NHS

Long-acting insulin - Brand names: Abasaglar, Lantus, Levemir, Semglee, Toujeo, Tresiba
Find out how long-acting insulin (insulin detemir, glargine and degludec) treats type 1 diabetes, type 2 diabetes and gestational diabetes, and how to take it.
Information: Levemir is being discontinued Levemir, a brand of long-acting insulin, is being discontinued. Supplies are expected to last until late 2026. If you currently take Levemir, you do not need to do anything. Your diabetes healthcare team will contact you to discuss switching to an alternative insulin.
