# Who can and cannot take tamsulosin - NHS

Who can and cannot take tamsulosin - Brand names: Contiflo XL, Faramsil MR, Flomaxtra XL, Pamsvax XL, Tabphyn MR Who can take tamsulosin Most adults aged 18 years and over can take tamsulosin. This medicine is not usually taken by children under 18 years old, unless a specialist children's doctor prescribes it. Who may not be able to take tamsulosin Tamsulosin is not suitable for some people. To make sure this medicine is safe for you, tell a doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to tamsulosin or other similar medicines, such as alfuzosin, doxazosin , prazosin or terazosin have orthostatic hypotension – a type of low blood pressure that can make you feel dizzy or light-headed when you stand up have severe liver or kidney problems are having eye surgery for cataracts or glaucoma ever faint while peeing or immediately afterwards (micturition syncope)
