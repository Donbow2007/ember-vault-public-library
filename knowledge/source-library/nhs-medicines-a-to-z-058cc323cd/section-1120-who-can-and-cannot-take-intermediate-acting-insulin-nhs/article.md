# Who can and cannot take intermediate-acting insulin - NHS

Who can and cannot take intermediate-acting insulin - Brand names: Humulin I, Insulatard Who can take intermediate-acting insulin Most adults and children with diabetes can take intermediate-acting insulin. Who may not be able to take intermediate-acting insulin Intermediate-acting insulin may not be suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you have ever had an allergic reaction to insulin or any other medicine.
