# Who can and cannot take nicorandil - NHS

Who can and cannot take nicorandil

Who can take nicorandil Most adults aged 18 and over can take nicorandil. Who may not be able to take nicorandil Nicorandil is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you have: ever had an allergic reaction to nicorandil or any other medicine low blood pressure (hypotension) a problem with fluid build-up in the lungs (pulmonary oedema) recently had a heart attack or been told you have heart failure low blood potassium levels and are taking potassium supplements kidney problems a genetic condition called glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency a digestive condition that affects the large intestine (diverticular disease)
