# Who can and cannot take levetiracetam - NHS

Who can and cannot take levetiracetam

Who can take levetiracetam Most adults and children aged 1 month and over can take levetiracetam. Who may not be able to take levetiracetam Levetiracetam is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to levetiracetam or any other medicine have kidney problems have had low mood (depression) or thoughts of harming or killing yourself are pregnant, or planning to get pregnant
