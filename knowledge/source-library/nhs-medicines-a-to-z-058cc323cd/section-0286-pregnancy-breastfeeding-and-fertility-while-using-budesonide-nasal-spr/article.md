# Pregnancy, breastfeeding and fertility while using budesonide nasal spray - NHS

Pregnancy, breastfeeding and fertility while using budesonide nasal spray - Brand name: Benacort Budesonide nasal spray and pregnancy Budesonide nasal spray can be used during pregnancy. Very little of the medicine gets into your bloodstream and almost none gets to your baby. Even if a small amount does get to your baby, it will not harm them. Budesonide nasal spray and breastfeeding It's OK to use budesonide nasal spray while breastfeeding. Only tiny amounts get into breast milk, so side effects in your baby are very unlikely. If you notice that your baby is not feeding as well as usual, or if you have any other concerns about your baby, talk to your health visitor, midwife, pharmacist or doctor as soon as possible. Budesonide nasal spray and fertility There's no evidence that using budesonide nasal spray reduces fertility in either men or women. Non-urgent advice: Tell your doctor if you're:
trying to get pregnant pregnant breastfeeding Information: Find out more about how treatment for allergic rhinitis such as budesonide might affect you and your baby on the Best Use of Medicines in Pregnancy (BUMPS) website.
