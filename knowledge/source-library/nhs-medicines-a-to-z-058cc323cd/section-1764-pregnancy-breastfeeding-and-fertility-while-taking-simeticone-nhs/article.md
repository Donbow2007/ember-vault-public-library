# Pregnancy, breastfeeding and fertility while taking simeticone - NHS

Pregnancy, breastfeeding and fertility while taking simeticone - Brand names: Wind-eze, WindSetlers, Infacol, Dentinox Colic Drops Simeticone and pregnancy Simeticone only works in your gut and does not get into your blood. It's safe to take if you are pregnant. Some products contain simeticone with other medicines or ingredients. Check with a doctor or pharmacist if you are unsure which type is safest in pregnancy. Simeticone and breastfeeding Simeticone is OK to take while breastfeeding. It does not get into breast milk. Some simeticone products contain other ingredients. Always check the ingredients and ask a pharmacist for advice. They will be able to recommend the best treatment for you. Simeticone and fertility There's no evidence that simeticone affects fertility in either men or women.
