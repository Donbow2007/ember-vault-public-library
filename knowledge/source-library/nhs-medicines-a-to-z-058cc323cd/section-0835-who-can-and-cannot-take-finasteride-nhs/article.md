# Who can and cannot take finasteride - NHS

Who can and cannot take finasteride - Brand names: Proscar, Propecia Who can take finasteride Most men aged 18 years old or over can take finasteride. It's generally not recommended for women or children. Who may not be able to take finasteride Finasteride is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell a doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to finasteride or any other medicine have ever had depression or thoughts about harming yourself have severe bladder problems have liver problems
