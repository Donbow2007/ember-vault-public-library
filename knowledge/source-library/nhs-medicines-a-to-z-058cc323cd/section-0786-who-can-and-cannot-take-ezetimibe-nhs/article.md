# Who can and cannot take ezetimibe - NHS

Who can and cannot take ezetimibe - Brand name: Ezetrol Who can take ezetimibe Most adults and children aged 10 years and over can take ezetimibe. Who may not be able to take ezetimibe Ezetimibe is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to ezetimibe or any other medicine have liver problems are pregnant, planning to get pregnant or are breastfeeding
