# Who can and cannot take allopurinol - NHS

Who can and cannot take allopurinol - Brand names: Zyloric, Uricto Who can take allopurinol Allopurinol can be taken by adults and sometimes children. Who may not be able to take allopurinol Allopurinol is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your pharmacist or doctor before taking it if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to allopurinol or any other medicine are of Han Chinese, Thai or Korean origin have problems with your liver or kidneys currently have an attack of gout have thyroid problems
