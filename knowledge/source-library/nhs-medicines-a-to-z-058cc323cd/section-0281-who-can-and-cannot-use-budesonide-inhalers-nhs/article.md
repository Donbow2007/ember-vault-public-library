# Who can and cannot use budesonide inhalers - NHS

Who can and cannot use budesonide inhalers - Brand names: Pulmicort, Budelin Who can use budesonide inhalers Most adults can use inhalers that contain budesonide for asthma or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Babies and children can use budesonide inhalers for asthma. Some brands may only be suitable for children aged 5 years old and over, and some may only be suitable for adults to use. Most adults can use budesonide in a nebuliser. Children aged under 18 years old can use budesonide in a nebuliser only if it's with advice from a specialist doctor. This is because children need to be monitored to make sure their condition is not getting worse. Who may not be able to use budesonide inhalers Budesonide inhalers are not suitable for some people. To make sure they're safe for you, tell a doctor or nurse if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to budesonide or any other medicine have ever had TB ( tuberculosis ) are taking or have recently taken other steroid medicines are pregnant or trying to get pregnant
