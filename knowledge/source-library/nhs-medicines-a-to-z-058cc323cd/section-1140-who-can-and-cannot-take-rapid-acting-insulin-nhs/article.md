# Who can and cannot take rapid-acting insulin - NHS

Who can and cannot take rapid-acting insulin - Brand names: Admelog, Apidra, Fiasp, Humalog, Lyumjev, NovoRapid, Trurapi Who can take rapid-acting insulin Most adults and children with diabetes can take rapid-acting insulin. Who may not be able to take rapid-acting insulin Rapid-acting insulin may not be suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you've ever had an allergic reaction to insulin or any other medicine.
