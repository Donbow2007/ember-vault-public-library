# Who can and cannot take amoxicillin - NHS

Who can and cannot take amoxicillin

Who can take amoxicillin Most adults and children can take amoxicillin. Information: Find out more about giving amoxicillin to children on the Medicines for Children website . Who may not be able to take amoxicillin Amoxicillin is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell a doctor before starting to take it if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to amoxicillin or penicillin or any other medicine have liver or kidney problems are due to have any blood or urine tests – taking amoxicillin can affect the results of some tests have recently had, or are due to have, any vaccinations have glandular fever – taking amoxicillin can cause a rash if you have this condition
