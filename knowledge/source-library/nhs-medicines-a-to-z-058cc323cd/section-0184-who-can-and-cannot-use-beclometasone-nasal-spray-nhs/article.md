# Who can and cannot use beclometasone nasal spray - NHS

Who can and cannot use beclometasone nasal spray - Brand names: Beconase, Nasobec, Pollenase Who can use beclometasone nasal spray Most adults and children aged 6 years old and over can use beclometasone nasal spray. Who may not be able to use beclometasone nasal spray Beclometasone nasal spray is not suitable for some people. To make sure it’s safe for you, tell your doctor before starting to use it if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to beclometasone or any other medicine are taking or have recently taken other steroid medicines have had nose surgery have an infection in your nose
