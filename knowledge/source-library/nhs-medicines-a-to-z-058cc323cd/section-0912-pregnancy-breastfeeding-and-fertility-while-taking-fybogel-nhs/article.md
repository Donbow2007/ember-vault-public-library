Pregnancy, breastfeeding and fertility while taking Fybogel

Fybogel and pregnancy Fybogel is commonly taken during pregnancy and is not harmful to your baby. Fybogel and breastfeeding You can take Fybogel while breastfeeding. It does not pass into breast milk so will have no effects on your baby. Fybogel and fertility There's no evidence to suggest that Fybogel reduces fertility in either men or women. For more information about how laxatives can affect you and your baby during pregnancy, visit the Best Use of Medicines in Pregnancy (BUMPS) website .
