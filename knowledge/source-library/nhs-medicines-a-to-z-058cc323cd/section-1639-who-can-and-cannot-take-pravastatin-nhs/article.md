# Who can and cannot take pravastatin - NHS

Who can and cannot take pravastatin

Who can take pravastatin Most adults and children over the age of 8 years can take pravastatin. Who may not be able to take pravastatin Pravastatin is not suitable for some people. Tell your doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to pravastatin or any other medicines have liver or kidney problems are trying to get pregnant, are pregnant, or you're breastfeeding have lung disease regularly drink large amounts of alcohol have an underactive thyroid have had a muscle disorder (including fibromyalgia ) you have a history of myasthenia gravis or ocular myasthenia
