# About Pepto-Bismol - NHS

About Pepto-Bismol - Other brand names: Pepti-Calm Bismuth subsalicylate is the main ingredient in Pepto-Bismol. This medicine is used for heartburn and acid reflux , indigestion , diarrhoea and feeling sick (nausea) . It works by forming a protective coating in your stomach, the lower part of your food pipe and your gut. Pepto-Bismol comes as tablets and a liquid that you swallow. You can buy it from pharmacies and shops. Key facts You can take Pepto-Bismol with or without food. It should work within 30 to 60 minutes. You can take up to 8 doses in 24 hours. Speak to a doctor if your symptoms are no better after 2 days. You're unlikely to get any side effects from Pepto-Bismol if you follow the instructions for taking it. If you're allergic to aspirin (or any of the ingredients in Pepto-Bismol) check with a pharmacist before taking this medicine.
