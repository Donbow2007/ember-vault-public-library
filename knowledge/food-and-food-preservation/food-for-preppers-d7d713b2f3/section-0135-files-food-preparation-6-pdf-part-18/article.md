ROLLS
2 quarts flour
2 tablespoons sugar
3 cups warm water
3 tablespoons lard
1 tablespoon salt
1 yeast cake
P ut sugar and yeast into warm water and let stand until
yeast rises to top. Sift flour and salt and rub in lard; add liquid
and mix, then knead until dough is smooth. Place in large bowl
and grease top with lard to prevent a crust forming, cover with
cloth and let rise in warm place until double its size (about 3
hours). Form into rolls or biscuit, grease with lard and let rise
second time (about 3 hours). Bake in a hot oven. This dough
will keep in the refrigerator several days if covered closely. When
dough has been kept in the refrigerator, allow more time for
second rising.
