EGGS BAKED IN TOMATOES
6 tomatoes
6 slices bread
6 eggs
Salt, pepper to taste
Butter
Select tomatoes of uniform size. Cut off stem end and take
out enough pulp to leave a space as large as an egg. Sprinkle
inside with salt and pepper and drop into each one an egg. Place
the filled tomatoes in a baking dish, with a little hot water, and
bake about twenty minutes, or until eggs are set and tomatoes
are a little softened. Serve tomatoes on rounds of bread browned
in butter.
