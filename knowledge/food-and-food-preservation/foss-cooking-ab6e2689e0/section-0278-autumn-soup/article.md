# Autumn Soup

You can cook the veggies on the stove instead of baking them, just pay more attention to avoid burning. You can also use pumpkin instead of squash. ⏲️ Prep time: 20 min 🍳 Cook time: 40 min 🍽️ Servings: 4 Ingredients 1 Medium Squash 3 Potatoes 4 Carrots 1 Small Onion 1 Chicken Boullion Cube 1 cup Water 2 cups Milk Salt Pepper Directions Peel and chop squash, potatoes, carrots, and onion, add to oven-safe pot. Add boullion and enough water to avoid burning (1 cup), place pot in oven (350F) to bake until veggies are soft. If there is too much stock, don’t be scared to pour some down the drain. Puree (I use electric beaters) while adding the milk. Add the salt and pepper, as desired.
