# Assam Tea

This is a simple Assam tea recipe. ⏲️ Prep time: 1 min 🍳 Cook time: 5 min 🍽️ Servings: 1 Ingredients 2 teaspoons of Assam Tea 150 ml of fresh water Sugar (Optional) Milk (Optional) Directions Add 150 ml of water to a small pan and bring it to boil. Then add 2 teaspoons of tea into it and reduce the heat to low. Strain the tea using a tea filter into a cup. Add sugar and milk to taste.
