# Oven Asado

Such a simple way to prepare such a juicy slab of meat- Argentinian style! ⏲️ Prep time: 10 min 🍳 Cook time: 45 min Ingredients Beef chuck short ribs (bone in preferrably) Celusal BBQ Salt - Iodized (including the specific type of salt due to smaller salt grains not working well) Directions Heat oven to 400F/200C Take beef out of fridge and let thaw or reach near room temperature [OPTIONAL] Rinse each end of the beef to get rid of any remaining bone fragments from when the meat was cut Pat all sides of beef dry with a paper towel Salt each side of the beef generously , salt will drip off of the meat due to the fat as the meat cooks Place beef on a rack and place in oven bone-side down , and be sure to add a tray below the rack to catch all the fat that drips off 20 minutes into cooking, flip each piece over (should be bone-side top now) After another 20 minutes, flip each piece again Take out of the oven after a total of 40 - 45 minutes, and let rest for 5 - 10 minutes
