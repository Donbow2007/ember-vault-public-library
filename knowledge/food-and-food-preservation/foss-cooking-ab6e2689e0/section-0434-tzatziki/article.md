# Tzatziki

A traditional greek dip that goes well with crackers, celery and carrots or in a greek gyro. Great for parties and sharing with your family friends. Can be eaten immediately after preparation or after a few hours of being in the fridge for enhanced flavor. ⏲️ Prep time: 2 min 🍳 Cook time: 6 min 🍽️ Servings: 3 Ingredients Greek yogurt, 2 cups Cucumber, 3 inches Dill Garlic, 1 clove/teaspoon Olive oil, 1 teaspoon Lemon/lime or vinegar Salt and pepper Directions Finely grate the cucumber and chop the dill Strain the cucumber thoroughly with a clean kitchen towel Mash the garlic and the olive oil in a mortar and pestle Add all three to the Greek yogurt Squeeze in some lemon/lime or vinegar Add a generous pinch of salt and small pinch of pepper Optionally add some extra olive oil Stir and enjoy with your favorite snack
