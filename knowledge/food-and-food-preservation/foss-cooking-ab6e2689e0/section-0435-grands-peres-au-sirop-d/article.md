# Grands-pères au sirop d

⏲️ Prep time: 5 min 🍳 Cook time: 15 min 🍽️ Servings:5-6 Ingredients 1 cup flour 2 TSP baking powder 1 pinch salt 2 TB butter, cut into pieces ½ cup milk 1 cup water 1 cup maple syrup Directions Stir together flour, baking powder and salt until aerated and no longer clumpy. Work in the butter with your fingertips and add milk. Form into 5-6 balls. In a saucepan equipped with a lid, bring water and syrup to a simmer. Add dough balls and cover. Simmer on low 12-15 minutes. Notes Avoid lifting the lid since these cook with the help of steam.
