# Menemen

Prep time: 10 min Cook time: 15 min Servings: 3 Ingredients 4 green peppers 4 tomatoes 4 eggs 2 onions butter 1 tsp of thyme (optional) 1 tsp of paprika (optional) Directions Boil some water and put the tomatoes in it and leave them for a couple of minutes Chop the onions and peppers into small pieces Take the tomatoes out of the water and peel them off Chop the tomatoes into 4 or 6 pieces Put the butter on the pan and wait till it’s melted Put onions and peppers on the pan You can add some salt (it will make them a little crispy) and stir till the onions are starting to get brown Add tomatoes and start mashing them with a spoon When the tomatoes are all mushed-up, add the eggs and seasonings Stir some more untill everything is mixed up well Cover up the pan so eggs don’t stay raw on the top It should be ready in 2 or 3 minutes.
