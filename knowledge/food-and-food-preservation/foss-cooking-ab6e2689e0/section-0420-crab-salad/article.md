# Crab salad

⏲️ Prep time: 10 min 🍳 Cook time: 30 min 🍽️ Servings: 3 Ingredients Сrab meat (300 grams) Egg (3) Rice (50 grams) Corn (1 can) Cucumber (1) Carrot (1) Onion (1) Mayonnaise (100 grams) Directions Boil rice, eggs and carrot Chop crab meat and all other ingredients Combine and mix all ingredients Add mayonnaise Chill before serving
