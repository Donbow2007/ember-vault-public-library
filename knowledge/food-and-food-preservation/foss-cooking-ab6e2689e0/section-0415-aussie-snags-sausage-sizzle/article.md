# Aussie Snags (sausage sizzle)

An Australian BBQ classic tradition that is simple and very easy to make. Great for parties and outdoor events
especially national events. Sometimes served at hardware chains. ⏲️ Prep time: 2 min 🍳 Cook time: 20 min 🍽️ Servings: Depends Ingredients Sausages (thin) Onions (4g per sausage) Tomato sauce Mustard (optional) White sliced bread Butter Oil Wood chips (optional, fuel for smokey flavour) Directions Chop up the onions into rings Bring the sausages to the grill, turning once every 5 mins Add oil of your choice to the hotplate then add the onions Use your tongs to toss the onions until they are golden brown When the onions are golden brown turn off the hotplate When the sausages are cooked, butter the bread, then the onions, and add a sausage (optionally split) Finally tomato sauce on top
