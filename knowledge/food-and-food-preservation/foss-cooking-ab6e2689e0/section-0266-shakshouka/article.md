# Shakshouka

Shakshouka is a dish of eggs poached in a sauce of tomatoes, olive oil, peppers, onion and garlic, and commonly spiced with cumin, paprika, cayenne pepper, and nutmeg.
This recipe is a quick and simple version for a delicious breakfast. ⏲️ Prep time: 10 min 🍳 Cook time: 10 min 🍽️ Servings: 2 Ingredients 5 small tomatoes 5 eggs 1 peeled white onion some chives cumin powdered sweet pepper Directions Scald the tomatoes so they are easier to peel. Peel the tomatoes and cut into cubes Chop the onion and glaze it on the frying pan with oil Add tomatoes SEASON Stir and wait for it to get denser Knock the eggs in Season the eggs
Cover the pan with a lid Cook until eggs are laid Add chives
