# Cinnamon Rolls

⏲️ Prep time: 2.5 hours 🍳 Cook time: 25 min 🍽️ Servings: 20 Ingredients For the dough: 1 cup Milk 2 teaspoons Instant Yeast 1/4 cup Sugar 2 Eggs 1/4 cup softened Butter 3 cups Flour A pinch of Salt For the filling: 1.5 cups Sugar 1/2 cup softened Butter 4 tablespoons ground Cinnamon Directions In a large bowl, mix all dough ingredients except butter. Kneed in the butter gradually until dough passes the “Windowpane Test”: Take a piece of the dough and stretch it gently apart, it should spread out smoothly until light is visible through the dough without tearing. This is a sign of good gluten development. Let rise for 90 minutes. In a small bowl, mix all filling ingredients. Take risen dough and roll it into a rectangle the size of your pan. Spread your filling inside your dough. Roll the dough into a log and cut into rolls. Place inside your pan and let rise for 30 minutes. Wash melted butter over your rolls and bake at 175°C. Drizzle frosting as soon as rolls come out of the oven, let cool, tear and serve.
