# Bolinhos de Coco

This is a Portuguese dish that translates to “coconut cupcakes,” though there are probably lots of equivalent dishes from other cultures.
Its simplicity and short preparation time make it perfect for a snack or dessert. ⏲️ Prep time: 5 min 🍳 Cook time: 10 min Ingredients 300 g coconut 150 g sugar 3 egg yolks 1 whole egg Powdered sugar (optional) Directions Mix the non-optional ingredients in a medium bowl. Scoop tablespoons of the batter into a cookie sheet or cupcake pan. Cook for around 10 minutes or until slightly brown on top. Top with powdered sugar if desired. Let cool and enjoy!
