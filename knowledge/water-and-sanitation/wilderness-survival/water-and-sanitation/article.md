Water and sanitation are essential for public health and preventable disease. Access to clean water and proper sanitation reduces the risk of waterborne diseases. The World Health Organization (WHO) recommends safe drinking water and proper sanitation for preventing disease. Inadequate water and sanitation can lead to outbreaks of diseases such as cholera, typhoid, and dysentery. The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) include access to safe water and sanitation as a key target.
