EDGEWOOD BATTERBREAD
This recipe, is a very old recipe, containing no baking powders.
1 cup meal
1 teaspoonful salt
1 heaping tablespoon butter
2 eggs
Sift meal and salt and add enough boiling water to mix thor­
oughly; beat the egg yolks and whites separately; mix meal with
the yolks; add butter, which must be melted, and beat well.
Then add the whites (beaten until very light) and enough milk
to make a batter the consistency of thin custard.
Heat the pan, place a teaspoon of butter in it and see th at the
pan is well greased, pour in the batter and if butter rises in the
mixture take a fork and run back and forth so as to go over the
top, bake in a hot oven but do not cook too rapidly.
— M r s . C h a s . E. B o l l in g .
