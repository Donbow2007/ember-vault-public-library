Boating Safety

1. Label your boat with your contact information and a visible marker for the location of the boat. This helps in identifying the boat if it is found adrift.

2. Keep your load light, evenly distributed, and low to reduce the center of gravity. During cold weather, take half your usual load to increase stability.

3. Know the maximum legal load for your boat and never exceed it, even under ideal conditions.

4. Reduce the number of people in the boat during rough water to improve stability.

5. Always wear a life jacket (PFD) and ensure that all passengers do the same. The majority of boating fatalities involve the use of alcohol or failure to wear a life jacket.
