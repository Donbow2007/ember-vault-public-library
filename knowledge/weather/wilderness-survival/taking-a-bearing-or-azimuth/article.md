To take a bearing or azimuth from point A to point B: place the edge of your compass along the line from point A to point B. Point the Direction of Travel arrow in the direction you will be walking. Align the orienteering lines on the compass dial with the north-south lines on your map. Ensure the orienting arrow points toward the north end of the magnetic north-south lines. The bearing is indicated at the point where the dial touches the back end of the Direction of Travel arrow, and the exact value is 50 degrees.
