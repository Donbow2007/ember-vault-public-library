The pole star is located at the tip of the Big Dipper's handle, not off the top of the constellation. Navigation by dead reckoning involves plotting and recording a series of courses measured by distance and direction from a starting point. Difficulties in navigation may occur due to factors such as poor landmarks, lack of visibility, or environmental conditions. The knowledgeable, skillful, and alert survivor can do well without a full complement of navigation aids. Constant awareness, logic, and training in nature's clues allow a survivor to determine general location even in the absence of detailed navigation aids. Careful planning and route selection before and during travel is essential.
