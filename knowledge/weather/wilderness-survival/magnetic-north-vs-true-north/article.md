Magnetic North vs True North: By convention, true (or geographical) north is usually at the top of the map. To take accurate magnetic bearings, draw magnetic north/south lines on your map using its declination information. Draw your first line carefully, and then draw additional parallel lines at equal distances across the map. The lines in the diagram slant 18.5 degrees to the left of vertical (West of true North) and are about ½ mile apart. They also make it easy to take magnetic compass bearings.
