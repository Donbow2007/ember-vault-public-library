Disaster Preparedness

Survival starts with an open mind and an awareness of the various situations that can occur. Once awareness develops, the next step is contingency planning, but for what type of disasters?

One size does not fit all. A wise prepper looks at historical disasters to determine which ones are most likely to happen next.

Californians know they should prepare for earthquakes, while people living in northern states should prepare for blizzards. Gulf Coast states experience numerous hurricanes, while western states frequently battle wildfires. A riot is more likely to occur in Chicago than in the small Missouri town of Sullivan. A terrorist act is more probable in New York City than in Boise.

However, a hazardous chemical spill, gas explosion, fire, or other similar incidents are more likely to occur in certain locations. Preparing for these types of disasters requires understanding local risks and taking appropriate precautions.
