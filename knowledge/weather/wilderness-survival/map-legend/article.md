The Map Legend typically includes scale, contour interval, location, magnetic declination, date drawn, and contour lines. Scale is defined as the distance across the map in kilometers, miles, and feet, with examples like 1:24,000. Contour interval refers to the difference in elevation between contour lines. Location includes latitude and longitude, map name, and surrounding maps. Magnetic declination is the difference between true north and magnetic north. Date drawn is the date the map was compiled, and it is important for topographic maps as they may have updated features after this date. Contour lines show the shape and elevation of land features, with spacing depending on slope steepness.
