To reverse your direction, add or subtract 180 degrees from your original bearing. This is known as finding your back-bearing or back-azimuth. Set the map on a flat, level surface, align the compass needle with the map's North-South arrow, and ensure the magnetic North-South lines are aligned. Objects on the map are oriented in the same directions as on the ground. The compass needle is magnetic and affected by iron/steel objects, such as knives, firearms, and vehicles, if they are too close to your compass.
