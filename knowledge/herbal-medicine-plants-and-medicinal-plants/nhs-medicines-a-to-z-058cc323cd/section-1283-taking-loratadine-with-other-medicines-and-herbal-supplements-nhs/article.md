# Taking loratadine with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Taking loratadine with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand name: Clarityn Cautions with other medicines Some medicines affect how loratadine works or can increase the chances of you having side effects. Tell your doctor if you're taking: amiodarone, a medicine used to treat an irregular heartbeat cimetidine, an indigestion medicine erythromycin , an antibiotic any medicine that makes you feel sleepy, gives you a dry mouth or makes it difficult for you to pee – taking loratadine might make these side effects worse Taking it with painkillers You can take loratadine together with painkillers such as paracetamol or ibuprofen . Mixing loratadine with herbal remedies or supplements There's very little information about taking herbal remedies and supplements with loratadine. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. However, there might be a problem with some of them, especially ones that cause sleepiness or a dry mouth, or make it difficult to pee. Ask your pharmacist for advice. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
