# Using hydrocortisone for piles and itchy bottom with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Using hydrocortisone for piles and itchy bottom with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand names: Anugesic-HC, Anusol HC, Proctosedyl, Uniroid, Xyloproct Cautions with other medicines Taking other medicines should not affect the way hydrocortisone treatments for piles and itchy bottom work. This includes prescribed medicines and ones you buy from a pharmacy or shop. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
