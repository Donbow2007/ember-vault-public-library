# Using fluticasone skin creams with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Using fluticasone skin creams with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand name: Cutivate Cautions with other medicines It's very unlikely that other medicines will affect the way fluticasone creams work. However, tell a pharmacist or doctor if you're taking: medicines used to treat HIV, such as ritonavir or cobicistat medicines used to treat fungal infections, such as ketoconazole or itraconazole other medicines that contain steroids, such as eczema creams, asthma inhalers, steroid tablets, injections, nasal sprays, or eye or nose drops Mixing fluticasone skin creams with herbal remedies and supplements There's not enough information to say that complementary medicines, herbal remedies and supplements are safe to take while using fluticasone. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
