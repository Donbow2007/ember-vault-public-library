# Taking lactulose with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Taking lactulose with other medicines and herbal supplements

Cautions with other medicines There are no known problems mixing lactulose with other medicines or herbal remedies. Taking lactulose with other laxatives For most people, 1 laxative will be enough to relieve constipation. Occasionally, you may need to take 2 different types of laxatives at the same time to get your bowels moving again. Only take 2 laxatives together on the advice of your doctor or pharmacist, as there's an increased risk of side effects. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
