# Taking Fybogel with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Taking Fybogel with other medicines and herbal supplements

Cautions with other medicines Fybogel can change the way some medicines work. Tell your doctor if you're taking any of these medicines before starting to take Fybogel: carbamazepine , a medicine for epilepsy and mood problems digoxin , a heart medicine mesalazine , a medicine to treat gut problems There's some evidence that Fybogel stops your body from taking in iron properly. If you need iron supplements, it's best to take them at least 1 hour before, or 4 hours after, you take Fybogel. Taking Fybogel with other laxatives For most people, 1 laxative will be enough to relieve constipation. Occasionally, you may need to take 2 different types of laxatives at the same time to get your bowels moving again. Only take 2 laxatives together on the advice of your doctor or pharmacist, as there's an increased risk of side effects. Taking Fybogel with irritable bowel syndrome remedies You can take Fybogel alongside irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) remedies such as Buscopan . Mixing Fybogel with herbal remedies and supplements There are no known problems with taking herbal remedies and supplements with Fybogel. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
