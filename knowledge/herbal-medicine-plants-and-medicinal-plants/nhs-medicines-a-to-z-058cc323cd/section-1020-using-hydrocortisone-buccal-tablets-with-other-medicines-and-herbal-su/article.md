# Using hydrocortisone buccal tablets with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Using hydrocortisone buccal tablets with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand name: Corlan Cautions with other medicines Taking other medicines, either prescribed or ones you buy from a pharmacy or shop, will not affect the way hydrocortisone buccal tablets work. Mixing hydrocortisone buccal tablets with herbal remedies and supplements There's very little information about taking herbal remedies and supplements with hydrocortisone buccal tablets. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
