# Having medroxyprogesterone contraceptive injections with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Having medroxyprogesterone contraceptive injections with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand names: Depo-Provera, Sayana Press Cautions with other medicines Other medicines will not usually affect how medroxyprogesterone contraceptive injections work. But tell your nurse or doctor when you go for the injection if you're taking other medicines. You can also talk to a pharmacist. Mixing medroxyprogesterone contraceptive injections with herbal remedies and supplements Do not take St John's wort, a herbal remedy used for depression, with medroxyprogesterone contraceptive injections. It can affect the way medroxyprogesterone works. There's not enough information to say that other complementary medicines, herbal remedies and supplements are safe to take with medroxyprogesterone contraceptive injections. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
