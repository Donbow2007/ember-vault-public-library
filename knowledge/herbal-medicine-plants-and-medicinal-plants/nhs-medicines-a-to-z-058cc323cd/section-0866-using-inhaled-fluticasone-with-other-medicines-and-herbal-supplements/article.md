# Using inhaled fluticasone with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Using inhaled fluticasone with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand name: Flixotide Cautions with other medicines Some medicines and fluticasone affect each other. This can increase your chance of side effects, or it may mean changing your dose of fluticasone. Check with a pharmacist or your doctor if you're taking: medicines used to treat HIV, such as ritonavir or cobicistat medicines to treat fungal infections , such as ketoconazole or itraconazole other medicines that contain steroids, such as eczema creams, other asthma inhalers, steroid tablets, injections, nasal sprays, eye or nose drops These medicines can increase the chances of side effects in the rest of your body. Mixing fluticasone with herbal remedies and supplements There is not enough information to say that complementary medicines and herbal remedies are safe to take with fluticasone. They are not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
