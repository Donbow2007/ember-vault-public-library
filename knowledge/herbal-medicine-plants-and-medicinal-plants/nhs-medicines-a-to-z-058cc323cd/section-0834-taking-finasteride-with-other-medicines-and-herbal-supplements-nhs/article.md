# Taking finasteride with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Taking finasteride with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand names: Proscar, Propecia Cautions with other medicines Finasteride is not affected by other medicines. Mixing finasteride with herbal remedies and supplements St John's wort (a herbal remedy sometimes taken to help with depression) might stop finasteride working as well as it's supposed to. There's not enough information to say that other complementary medicines, herbal remedies and supplements are safe to take with finasteride. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
