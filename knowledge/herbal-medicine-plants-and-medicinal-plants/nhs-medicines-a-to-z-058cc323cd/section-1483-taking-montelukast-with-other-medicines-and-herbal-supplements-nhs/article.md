# Taking montelukast with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Taking montelukast with other medicines and herbal supplements - Brand name: Singulair Cautions with other medicines There are some medicines that may affect how montelukast works. Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any of these medicines: epilepsy medicines such as phenobarbital or phenytoin rifampicin, for tuberculosis and some other infections gemfibrozil, for high cholesterol It's usually safe to take everyday painkillers with montelukast. However, do not take non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as aspirin or ibuprofen if they've ever made your asthma symptoms worse. Mixing montelukast with herbal remedies and supplements There's not enough information to say that complementary medicines and herbal remedies are safe to take with montelukast. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
