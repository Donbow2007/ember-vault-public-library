# Using fentanyl with other medicines and herbal supplements - NHS

Using fentanyl with other medicines and herbal supplements

Cautions with other medicines There are some medicines that may affect how fentanyl works and increase the chance that you'll have side effects. Tell your doctor if you're taking any medicines: to help you sleep for high blood pressure for an irregular heartbeat to help stop you feeling or being sick to treat symptoms of an allergy for mental health problems including depression, or to reduce stress or anxiety for any infection (including HIV ) to control fits or seizures Taking fentanyl with other painkillers It's safe to take fentanyl with paracetamol , ibuprofen or aspirin . Do not take any painkillers containing codeine including co-codamol , ibuprofen and codeine (Nurofen Plus) or Solpadeine when using fentanyl. You'll be more likely to get side effects if you take any of these while using fentanyl. Mixing fentanyl with herbal remedies and supplements There's not enough information to say that complementary medicines and herbal remedies are safe to take with fentanyl. They're not tested in the same way as pharmacy and prescription medicines. They're generally not tested for the effect they have on other medicines. Important:
Medicine safety
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you're taking any other medicines, including herbal remedies, vitamins or supplements.
