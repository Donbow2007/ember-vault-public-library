The four most important factors when starting a fire are spark, tinder, fuel, and oxygen. The most common ways to create a spark include: 1. Strike-anywhere matches, 2. Cigarette lighters, 3. Flint and steel, 4. Electric sparks. Sunlight-based methods, such as magnifying glasses and Fresnel lenses, are effective when the sun is strong and can be used on sunny days. However, they are limited to conditions where sunlight is available and not effective in adverse weather conditions.
