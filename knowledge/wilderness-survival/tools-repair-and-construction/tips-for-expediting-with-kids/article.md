Tips for Expediting with Kids in Maine Woods:

1. **Use Technology for Safety**: If children will be in the Maine woods, take photos of them with clothing and footwear to assist warden searches.
2. **Stay Safe and Confident**: Teach children not to wander if lost. If they see an open area, guide them to it and stay there. Encourage them to answer calls from adults.
3. **Act Quickly in Emergencies**: If a child is suspected to be lost, immediately notify 9-1-1. Emergency responders may turn around, but delays can make the situation more difficult.
4. **Maintain Calm and Support**: Remind children that parents will not be angry and are always happy to ensure their safety.
