Woods Travel Tips

### Follow a Logging Road

To navigate through Maine woods, follow a logging road if you encounter one. Logging roads are often used by bulldozers to push trees into the deep woods, causing them to lean in the direction of the deep woods. To find your way out, walk opposite the direction of the tree lean or against the treetops. If you find an active logging road, stay on it to increase your chances of being found.

### Follow a Brook

You can also follow a brook downstream. While the brook may eventually lead to the Atlantic Ocean, you may encounter a camp, road, or town before reaching the ocean. Traveling near water courses increases the risk of getting wet due to dense growth. Be aware of this when planning your route.
