Improvised lenses can be used to start fires, but their effectiveness varies depending on the type. Reading glasses can be used by beading water on the lens to create a more effective focal spot. Binoculars are more effective for starting fires by pointing at the sun and finding the focal spot. Monoculars and telescopes are similar but with different designs. Rifle scopes are tailored for firearms. The canonical article provides a detailed overview of these lenses and their applications.
