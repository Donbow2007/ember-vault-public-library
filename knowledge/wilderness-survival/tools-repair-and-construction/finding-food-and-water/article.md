In a wilderness survival situation, it is not advisable to live on less than 2,000 calories per day, as this can lead to severe health issues. Water is more critical than food, and a healthy individual should aim for at least 2-3 liters of water daily. Survival requires a balanced intake of food and water, with the body needing adequate hydration and nutrition. In areas with no surface water, it is essential to dig for water, and in winter, look for water under ice. Stagnant water should be boiled or treated with iodine or halazone tablets. Dew, rainwater, and fish juices can also be sources of water.
