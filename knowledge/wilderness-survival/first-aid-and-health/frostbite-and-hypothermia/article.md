Frostbite cases require immediate medical attention as soon as the individual is out of the cold. Thawing tissue before professional treatment can lead to severe complications. Shivering is a natural response to cold and can be beneficial, but it should not be ignored. It is important to monitor for signs of hypothermia and seek medical care promptly. Shivering should not be used as a sole indicator of hypothermia, as it can be a normal physiological response. In cases of immersion hypothermia, rebound may occur, requiring repeated rewarming. Always seek medical help immediately if frostbite or hypothermia is suspected.
