Deer ticks are vectors for Lyme Disease, Anaplasmosis, and Babesiosis. They are most active in June and July and can transmit these diseases through nymphs or females. To prevent tick bites, use tick repellent containing permethrin, applied to clothing and packs, but not on skin. Repellents with DEET are effective on skin surfaces. Ticks typically attach to legs, skin, and hair lines, and can also attach to armpits and groin. Check for ticks daily in wooded areas. If a tick is not engorged, remove and dispose of it. If engorged, remove it and place it in a container for healthcare provider reference or take a photo.
