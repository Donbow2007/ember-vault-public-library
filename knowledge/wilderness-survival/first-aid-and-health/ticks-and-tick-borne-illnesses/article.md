Ticks can be active throughout the year, but their activity is most pronounced in the spring and summer when there is little or no snow cover. Dog ticks (also called wood ticks) are common in Maine and are generally not disease-transmitting when they embed in a person, but they can cause infections. Tick-borne illnesses, such as Lyme disease, are transmitted through the bite of infected ticks. Prevention includes using insect repellent, wearing long sleeves and pants, and checking for ticks after being outdoors.
