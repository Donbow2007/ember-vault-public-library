Wilderness Survival: First Aid & Health

When journeying into the wilderness, it is essential to carry a complete first aid kit and book. It is also wise to take a first aid course. A good diet, cleanliness, and appropriate clothing will lower the risk of harmful situations.

Disease, infection, and insect bites can be avoided with a proper diet and good hygiene. It is important to bathe daily, but if this is not possible, wash hands frequently. Soap can be made using ashes and animal fat or by boiling the inner bark of a pine tree. A toothbrush can be constructed by mashing the end of a green twig.

When setting out for your journey, pack a wide range of clothing and extra footwear. A complete first aid kit should include items such as bandages, antiseptics, pain relievers, and other essentials. Additionally, it is recommended to carry a first aid course certificate and a well-stocked kit to handle various emergencies in the wilderness.
