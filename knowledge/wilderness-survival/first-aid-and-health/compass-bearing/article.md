The compass bearing procedure involves determining the direction to an object using a compass. To take a bearing on an object, place the compass flat and point the Direction of Travel arrow at the object. Rotate the compass dial until the north end of the orienting arrow is directly beneath the north end of the compass needle. The bearing is read from the dial where it touches the back end of the Direction of Travel arrow. The example of '50 degrees' should be explained in the source text.
