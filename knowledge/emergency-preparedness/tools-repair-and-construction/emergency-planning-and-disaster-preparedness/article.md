Emergency Planning and Disaster Preparedness

Emergency planning and disaster preparedness involve the systematic preparation for potential hazards and emergencies. Key steps include:
1. **Hazard Identification**: Understand the types of hazards that may affect you and your community.
2. **Emergency Plan Development**: Create a plan that outlines actions to take during emergencies.
3. **Disaster Supplies Kit**: Collect and assemble essential supplies for emergencies.
4. **Community Warning Systems**: Learn about and utilize community warning systems and evacuation routes.
5. **Specific Hazard Protocols**: Know what to do for specific types of hazards.
6. **Practice and Maintenance**: Regularly practice and maintain your emergency plan to ensure readiness.
