Be Prepared for Any Disaster

Disaster preparedness involves learning how to prepare for, stay safe during, and recover from disasters. This guide provides essential information on these three key areas:

1. **Preparation**: Learn about potential hazards, emergency plans, and resources.
2. **Safety During Disasters**: Understand how to stay safe during emergencies, including evacuation procedures and communication strategies.
3. **Recovery**: Know how to rebuild and restore after a disaster, including accessing support and resources.

The guide references FEMA P-2143, a comprehensive document that covers all aspects of disaster preparedness. It is recommended to consult this resource for detailed information on specific hazards and response strategies.
