Frostbite occurs when tissue freezes from exposure to elements or high wind. First-degree frostbite turns the area cold, white, and numb; it can be treated by cupping and blowing to warm it. Second-degree frostbite causes blisters, while third and fourth degrees result in irreversible damage. Frostbite can be avoided with adequate clothing. Superficial frostbite is treated by cupping and blowing, but deep frostbite requires medical attention.
