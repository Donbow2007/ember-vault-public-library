Boots are categorized into three main types: all-rubber, all-leather, and leather/fabric combinations. All-rubber boots are waterproof but lack ventilation and ankle support. All-leather or combination leather-fabric boots offer good ankle support and high-traction soles. Rubber-bottomed, leather-topped boots provide ventilation, ankle support, warmth, and waterproofing. To manage moisture, felt inserts can help draw moisture away from the feet, but silicone sealing should be avoided if ventilation is important. For all-leather or combination boots, felt inserts are recommended for moisture management without compromising ventilation.
