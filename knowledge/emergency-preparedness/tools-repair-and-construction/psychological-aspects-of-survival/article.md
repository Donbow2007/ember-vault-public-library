Psychological aspects of survival are as important as environmental factors. Stress is a natural reaction to pressure and essential for survival, but too much stress can be harmful. Survivors must recognize and manage psychological stresses to survive. Stress can highlight the significance of events in a person's life, but it should be managed to avoid excessive harm. The goal is to have stress, but not an excess of it. Too much stress can lead to distress, which causes uncomfortable tension that we try to escape and avoid.
