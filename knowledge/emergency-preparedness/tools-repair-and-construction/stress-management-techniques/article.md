Stress management techniques are essential for survival and maintaining focus in challenging situations. Military training is a key method for developing stress management skills. Techniques to develop include relaxation, time management, assertiveness, and the ability to control how you view a situation. Stress can lead to negative outcomes such as depression, inattention, and loss of confidence. The 'will to survive' is a personal choice and not necessarily a result of stress. This distinction should be made to avoid confusion.
