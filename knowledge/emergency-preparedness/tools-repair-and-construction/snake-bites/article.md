Snake bites are not overly common in British Columbia. Rattlesnakes are found in the dry belt of the southern interior. If you encounter a snake, slowly ease back. Snake bites rarely cause death, and victims may require immediate medical attention. The following steps should be taken: 1. Keep the person calm and reassure them that treatment is available. 2. Restrict movement and keep the affected area just below heart level to reduce venom flow. 3. Remove rings or constricting items and create a loose splint. 4. Monitor vital signs and seek medical help immediately.
