Preppers are not doomsdayers. They are strategic planners who look ahead, anticipate natural and man-made disasters, and prepare logically for them. Survival begins with an open mind and the acknowledgment that 'life happens.' Preppers recognize that crises, whether natural or man-made, are inevitable and focus on preparation rather than opposition. The American Red Cross responds to approximately 70,000 disasters annually, including both natural and man-made events.
