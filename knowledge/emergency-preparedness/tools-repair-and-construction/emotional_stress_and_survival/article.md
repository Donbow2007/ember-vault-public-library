Survivors face significant emotional stress, and depression can be a major psychological challenge. While some may view depression as a normal part of the survival process, others emphasize its negative impact and the need for psychological resilience. Survivors must maintain a sense of humor and perspective, and depression can lead to physical and mental health issues. Loneliness and boredom are common and can contribute to psychological distress. The article should clarify the dual nature of depression and emphasize the importance of mental health and resilience in survival.
