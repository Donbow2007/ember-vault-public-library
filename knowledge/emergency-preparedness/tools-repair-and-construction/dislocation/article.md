Dislocation occurs when ligaments around a joint tear, allowing the bone to move from its socket. It is important to treat dislocation only with trained professionals to prevent permanent damage. The affected limb should be supported with a sling and pain managed with aspirin. Sprains are treated by applying cold for 24 hours, then heat after swelling subsides, and splinted until pain disappears. Concussions may cause nose or ear leakage, and require warm care, pain management, and rest.
