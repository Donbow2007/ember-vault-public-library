Rest is a basic factor for recovery from fatigue and is also important in resisting further fatigue. Survivors should rest before output shows a definite decline and longer rest breaks may be required. Fatigue can be reduced by working 'smarter' through adjusting the pace of effort and the technique of work. Rest breaks providing relaxation are the most effective in mental work, but also mention that rest breaks providing relaxation are the most effective in physical work. A survivor must avoid complete exhaustion and distinguish between exhaustion and being uncomfortably tired.
