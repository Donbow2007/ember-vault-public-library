Fire starters can be created using household items like cotton rope, newspaper strips, and paper towels. A single cotton ball can be used to light two fires by soaking it in petroleum jelly or wax. These fire starters are best practiced at home before being used in survival situations. Shaving sticks are effective as fire starters; to create one, take a dry stick and whittle it, holding the shavings in place. This process allows for rapid fire starting and is a skill best practiced at home first. Fire making involves using kindling, such as birch bark and dry limbs, and protecting it from rain until ready to use.
