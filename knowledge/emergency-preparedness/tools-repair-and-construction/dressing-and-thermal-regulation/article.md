Dressing for thermal regulation is essential for maintaining body temperature. Layering is recommended for maintaining warmth without sweating. A thin, wicking layer of polypropylene or a similar synthetic should be worn closest to the skin to move moisture away from the body. Avoid cotton. The next layer should be an insulating material that traps warm air, such as wool or fleece. On top of that, a breathable, waterproof shell layer like Gore-Tex is recommended to allow vapor escape while keeping wind and rain out.
