Injury, illness, and death are real possibilities a survivor has to face. Thirst and dehydration are among the most critical problems facing survivors and can be tolerated if managed properly. However, they must be addressed carefully to avoid complications. Pain is a warning signal that the body uses to protect itself from harm and can be controlled during survival. Concentration and intense effort can help reduce pain during survival tasks. A survivor must understand the importance of controlling stress associated with vulnerability to injury, illness, and death.
