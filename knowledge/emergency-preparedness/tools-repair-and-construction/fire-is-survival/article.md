Fire is a vital tool for survival, enabling warmth, companionship, and protection from danger. It is essential to control and extinguish fires before leaving camp to ensure safety. Appropriate gear like mylar blankets and contractor trash bags can provide warmth, but they should not be relied upon once a fire is started. Starting a fire is a high priority during daylight, and keeping busy with firewood gathering helps combat boredom, worry, and panic. Fire has been a cornerstone of human survival for thousands of years, and its use is a positive action that supports comfort and security.
