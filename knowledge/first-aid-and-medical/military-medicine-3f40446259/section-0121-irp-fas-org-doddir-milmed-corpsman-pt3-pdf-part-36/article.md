DESQUAMATE—To shed, peel, or scale off.

DIATHERMY—The generation of heat in tissue by electric current for medical or

DISINFECTION—The killing of infectious agents outside the body by physical or
chemical means applied directly.
concurrent—Done during the treatment of a patient with a communicable

terminal—Done after a patient has been discharged or transferred.
DISINFESTATION—Aphysical or chemical means of destroying animal or insect

ELIXIR—An aromatic, sweetened, hydroalcoholic solution containing medicinal

EMBOLUS—A clot or other plug brought by the blood from another vessel and

EMETIC—A substance that causes vomiting.
EMOLLIENT—A drug that softens, soothes, or smooths the skin or irritated

EMULSION—A liquid preparation containing two unmixable liquids, such as oil

ENCAPSULATED—Enclosed within a capsule.
ENDEMIC—The constant presence of a disease in a given locality.
ENTERIC—Of or within the intestine.
EPIDEMIC—The outbreak of disease in a geographic area in excess of normal
