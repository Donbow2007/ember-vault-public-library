Who can and cannot take co-codamol for children

Who can take co-codamol Most children and young people aged 12 years old and over can take co-codamol if other everyday painkillers have not worked. Who may not be able to take co-codamol Co-codamol is not suitable for some children. To make sure it's safe for your child, tell their doctor or a pharmacist before giving them co-codamol if your child has: ever had an allergic reaction to co-codamol or any other medicine lung problems or breathing difficulties a head injury adrenal gland problems a condition that causes fits or seizures liver problems had their tonsils or adenoids removed because of a sleep problem called obstructive sleep apnoea
