# Who can and cannot take or use docusate - NHS

Who can and cannot take or use docusate - Brand names: Dulcoease, Dioctyl, Docusol, Norgalax Who can take or use docusate Most adults and children aged 12 years and over can take docusate.

Important

Never give docusate to a child under 12 years old unless their doctor prescribes it or a pharmacist recommends it. Who may not be able to take or use docusate Docusate is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor or pharmacist before starting to take it if you have: ever had an allergic reaction to docusate or any other medicine a blockage in your gut (intestine) stomach pains been feeling sick or have vomited in the last 24 hours Do not use a docusate enema if you have: piles (haemorrhoids) or bleeding from your bottom a tear or sore around your anus ( anal fissure ) ulcerative colitis , Crohn's disease or any other inflammatory bowel condition paralytic ileus (when the muscles in your bowel are not able to move food and liquid along)
