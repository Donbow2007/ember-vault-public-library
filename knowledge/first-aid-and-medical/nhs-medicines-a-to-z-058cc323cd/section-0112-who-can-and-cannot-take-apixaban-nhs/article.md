# Who can and cannot take apixaban - NHS

Who can and cannot take apixaban

Who can take apixaban Most adults aged 18 and over can take apixaban. Who may not be able to take apixaban Apixaban is not suitable for some people. To make sure it's safe for you, tell your doctor if you: have ever had an allergic reaction to apixaban or any other medicine are trying to get pregnant or are already pregnant – apixaban can be harmful to your baby have liver or kidney problems have had a recent spinal injury or surgery have any injuries that are currently bleeding a lot, such as a wound have a stomach ulcer have antiphospholipid syndrome , a condition that affects the immune system and makes you more likely to get blood clots have ever had surgery to replace a heart valve
