# Who can and cannot take isosorbide mononitrate and isosorbide dinitrate - NHS

Who can and cannot take isosorbide mononitrate and isosorbide dinitrate - Brand names: Chemydur XL, Isotard, Monomil XL, Elantan LA, Isoket Retard Who can take isosorbide mononitrate and isosorbide dinitrate Most adults aged 18 years old and over can take isosorbide mononitrate or isosorbide dinitrate. Who may not be able to take isosorbide mononitrate and isosorbide dinitrate Isosorbide mononitrate and isosorbide dinitrate are not suitable for some people. To make sure they're safe for you, tell your doctor or pharmacist if you have: ever had an allergic reaction to isosorbide mononitrate or isosorbide dinitrate or any other medicine any other heart problems, including a recent heart attack any recent brain or head injuries or problems ever had a stroke an underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism) low blood pressure (hypotension) anaemia glaucoma
